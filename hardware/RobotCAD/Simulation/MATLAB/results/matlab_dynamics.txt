   Why the hand calc is optimistic: it gives each drive wheel 42 N. With the preloaded spring the casters carry
   about 27 N between them, so each drive wheel has 29 N and the traction is about 41 N, not 59 N. At a square
   edge the leading caster needs a push of its vertical load x tan(65 deg), about 2.1 x 16 N, and that load grows
   as the carriage compresses, so the drive wheels slip at the edge. More carriage travel alone does not help;
   a softer, lighter spring does, because it leaves more weight on the drive wheels and less on the caster.
   The 3-inch caster lowers the edge angle (tan 53 deg) but with the design spring it still stalls.
